My contribution

Weapon hit reactions

I added local physical-animation reactions and short staggers for infantry, with different settings per weapon. Grenade explosions push enemies away with distance-based falloff. The reaction also cancels a queued melee hit.

HUD and menus

I built the health and ammunition display, weapon slots, minimap, and title, pause and result menus. I connected volume and language settings to the interface and added English/Korean text resources.

Gamepad input and integration

I added FPS gamepad mappings and device-specific prompts, and prevented gameplay input from leaking through cinematics. I also resolved source conflicts while integrating my branch with changes from the team.

Checking combat behavior

In a PIE test, I fired a grenade through the normal weapon path and checked damage and knockback at different distances. I also checked that hit reactions cancel queued attacks and that death and corpse recovery can still follow the reaction.

Team scope and development tools

The infantry reactions, interface and input described here were my areas of work. Teammates developed the fortress locomotion and core enemy and recovery systems. The game is a single-player jam build. The combat checks above cover infantry and specific test scenarios.

We used AI assistance during development. The itch.io page declares its use in code, graphics and text. The implementation and checks on this page describe my assigned work within that project.
